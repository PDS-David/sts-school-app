## Policy gate

SS3 is examination-preparation only. The permitted third-year work is revision of already-taught material, WAEC/NECO/JAMB syllabus-aligned past-question practice, mock examinations, marking, feedback and reassessment. No new instructional topics may be introduced as SS3 lessons.

The supplied legacy archive is evidence of historical source content, not authority to override this policy. In particular, several second-term SS3 documents contain substantive instructional units. Those units must not be imported as new SS3 teaching lessons. They may be used only to identify previously taught examinable content and create revision/practice material when the exact external syllabus or past-question source is verified. Unverified content stays blocked; do not invent replacement topics or questions.

## Immediate disposition

1. Treat the SS3 archive as a source corpus to audit, not an import-ready curriculum.
2. Build a subject-by-subject register identifying every scheme item and weekly body section as one of: `REVISION`, `PAST_QUESTION_PRACTICE`, `MOCK`, `FEEDBACK/REASSESSMENT`, `NEW_INSTRUCTION_BLOCKED`, or `UNRESOLVED`.
3. For each permitted practice item, capture the examining body, syllabus/version, year, paper, question number, exact source location, marks, answer key/marking scheme and rights/provenance.
4. Do not transform a new instructional topic into an invented “revision” question. Prior-teaching evidence is required.
5. Reconcile duplicated Book Keeping sources and ambiguous subject files (including generic English/French/Food and Nutrition filenames) to their actual term, class and provenance.
6. The archive contains only first- and second-term folders; it is not evidence for a third-term source set.
