## Verified external evidence

| Existing blocker | Evidence established | Safe curriculum consequence | Still required before closure |
|---|---|---|---|
| `AG2-T2-FLORICULTURE-SPECIES` | Kew's Plants of the World Online (POWO) accepts *Ixora coccinea* and describes it as a shrub. The genus *Ixora* includes shrubs and trees, so a genus-only name cannot always support a single growth-form classification. | Do not classify an ambiguous common/genus name as a fixed tree/shrub without identifying the species. Retain disputed source entries as unresolved rather than silently rewriting them. | Match every school-source common name to an identified botanical taxon; verify all names and growth forms individually. Confirm corrections with the subject lead. |
| `AG2-T2-DISEASE-TAXONOMY` | The American Phytopathological Society (APS) explains that Oomycota are not true fungi. APS identifies *Phytophthora megakarya* as the cause of cacao black pod. | Retain the corrected statement that *Phytophthora* is an oomycete. Do not place it in the true-fungi group merely because older school classifications call it a fungus. | Review each remaining disease/pathogen/vector pair against crop-specific plant-pathology references; distinguish symptoms from pathogen signs and confirm local crop context. |
| `AG2-T2-PEST-TABLE` and `AG2-T2-PEST-CONTROL` | FAO's IPM guidance calls for regular monitoring of pests and beneficial organisms, consideration of non-chemical controls, crop-specific pesticide registration, label-compliant rates/timing/pre-harvest intervals, trained applicators and safe storage/use. | Remove obsolete trade-name prescriptions from student-facing guidance. Teach identification, monitoring and integrated pest management first; pesticide use is conditional on current legal registration and label directions. | Identify every pest and damage entry in the original table. A Nigeria-specific registration check is still needed for any named pesticide/product; this register does not approve any product. |
| `AG2-T3-WEED-NOMENCLATURE` | Kew POWO provides accepted-name and synonym records, including Nigeria-native taxa. This is an appropriate taxonomic reference, but each school-source entry must be matched to a precise taxon. | Do not infer a botanical name from a similar-looking common name. Keep uncertain names unresolved. | Reconcile each common name and printed botanical spelling to a voucher-quality identification or authoritative flora/taxonomic record; preserve the original wording and document every correction. |
| `AG2-T3-FEED-THRESHOLD-ANTIBIOTICS` | WHO recommends against routine use of antibiotics to promote growth or prevent disease in healthy food-producing animals. APS/USDA-linked plant pathology sources confirm the oomycete distinction above; no single universal roughage fibre threshold is established by the evidence gathered here. | Keep antibiotics out of the nutrient/feed-class list. Do not teach an unqualified universal fibre percentage; explain that feed classification thresholds depend on the adopted analytical system. | Subject lead must decide whether the source's antibiotic passage is omitted or treated separately as veterinary-medicine context. Cite a named feed-analysis/classification system before retaining a numeric fibre threshold. |
| `AG2-T3-ACCOUNTING-EXAMPLE` | The package records an internal inconsistency: the source data list ₦2,000 as egg sales while the printed profit-and-loss table labels the receipt as spent-layer sales. | This is a source-transcription issue, not something an external reference can resolve. Do not silently substitute one label in the assessed model answer. | Inspect the original printed page and obtain teacher confirmation. Correct the table transcription only after that comparison. |
