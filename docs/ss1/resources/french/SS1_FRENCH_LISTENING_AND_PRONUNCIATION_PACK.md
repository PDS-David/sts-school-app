## 3. Original listening passage — À la bibliothèque

**Level:** SS1 beginner  
**Skills:** Listening for gist and detail, polite requests, school vocabulary, numbers/time references and turn-taking  
**Voices:** Two (Ada and the librarian)  
**Suggested duration:** 40–60 seconds at a clear beginner pace

### Teacher/recording script

**Ada :** Bonjour, monsieur. Je cherche un livre sur les animaux.

**Le bibliothécaire :** Bonjour. Regardez la deuxième étagère, à gauche.

**Ada :** Merci. Est-ce que je peux emprunter ce livre ?

**Le bibliothécaire :** Oui. Avez-vous votre carte ?

**Ada :** Oui, la voici. Quand dois-je rendre le livre ?

**Le bibliothécaire :** Vendredi prochain.

**Ada :** Très bien. Merci et au revoir.

**Le bibliothécaire :** Au revoir.

### Vocabulary support

| French | English |
|---|---|
| une bibliothèque | a library |
| un bibliothécaire / une bibliothécaire | a librarian |
| chercher | to look for |
| un livre | a book |
| une étagère | a shelf |
| à gauche | on the left |
| emprunter | to borrow |
| rendre | to return |
| ouvrir | to open |
| fermer | to close |
| la voici | here it is (feminine item) |
| vendredi prochain | next Friday |

### Listening questions — 10 marks

1. What is Ada looking for? (1)
2. What is the book about? (1)
3. Where should Ada look? (1)
4. Which shelf should she use? (1)
5. Is the shelf on the left or right? (1)
6. What does Ada ask permission to do? (1)
7. What does the librarian ask her to show? (1)
8. When must Ada return the book? (1)
9. Give one polite expression used by Ada. (1)
10. How does the conversation end? (1)

### Answer key

1. A book.
2. Animals.
3. On the shelves.
4. The second shelf.
5. On the left.
6. She asks to borrow the book.
7. Her library card.
8. Next Friday.
9. « Merci » or « Très bien » in context; « s’il vous plaît » is not in this version of the dialogue.
10. Ada says goodbye and the librarian replies « Au revoir ».

### Teacher administration

1. First reading: read the dialogue once at natural but measured speed. Students do not write; they identify the setting and speakers.
2. Second reading: read it again. Students answer questions 1–10.
3. Third reading (optional): pause after each speaker turn and allow students to repeat.
4. Pair activity: students perform Ada and the librarian, then exchange roles.
5. Oral observation: assess pronunciation, intelligibility, pace, turn-taking and response without reading. Record this separately from the written listening score.

## 4. Pronunciation and oral practice

Practise these words in short groups before reading the dialogue:

- bibliothèque
- bibliothécaire
- français
- deuxième
- étagère
- gauche
- emprunter
- vendredi
- aujourd’hui

Teacher cues:

- Keep the final consonant silent where standard pronunciation requires it (for example, the final consonant in *français* is not pronounced as a separate final /s/ sound in ordinary speech).
- Practise the French *r* gently; do not force an English /r/.
- Keep the vowel distinction in *vous* and *vu* audible.
- Link words naturally where liaison is required, but do not insert liaison everywhere.
- Students should imitate a clear model and prioritise intelligibility over exaggerated accent imitation.

The teacher should model the words and assess actual student production. A printed phonetic guide alone is not evidence of oral competence.

## 5. Recording specification for the school

If the school wants an audio file for the application, record the original passage above. Recommended production settings:

- Two distinct adult voices, or one teacher voicing both roles with clearly different delivery.
- French pronunciation; moderate beginner-friendly pace.
- WAV master: 44.1 kHz, 16-bit, mono; export delivery MP3 at 128–192 kbps.
- No background music, sound effects, or noise reduction that distorts consonants.
- Leave a short pause between speaker turns and a longer pause before the comprehension questions if producing an exercise version.
- Keep a transcript alongside the recording.
- Record the speaker, date, language/accent model, editor, file duration, file size, MIME type and SHA-256 checksum in the asset manifest.
- Obtain consent from any identifiable voice performer and retain the consent record.

**Suggested asset names:** `ss1-french-t3-library-dialogue-fr-v1.wav` and `ss1-french-t3-library-dialogue-fr-v1.mp3`.

This document is the authored source script and teaching pack; it is not itself an audio recording. The application must not advertise an MP3 as installed until an actual audio file has been recorded, checked and added to the asset manifest.
