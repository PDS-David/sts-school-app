# BIO-SCOPE-T2-W06-BIOMES — scope disposition

Status: **IMPLEMENTED — SCOPE FINDING RECORDED**.

## Source comparison
- Week 6: the content list includes “Biome”; the Week 6 body contains a short definition at converted DOC block 527: a biome is a large natural terrestrial ecosystem.
- Week 7: the scheme is explicitly titled “Biomes”; its body teaches the definition, local Nigerian biomes, world biomes, vegetation patterns and climatic controls.

## Finding
Week 6 is best treated as an **introductory mention / vocabulary preview** of the term biome, not as a second full biome lesson. Week 7 is the main teaching week for the detailed biome topic. This interpretation avoids duplicating Week 7's full content while preserving the Week 6 content-list and definition.

## Delivery recommendation
- Week 6: retain a short definition and a cross-reference to the forthcoming Week 7 detailed lesson.
- Week 7: teach local and world biomes, climatic controls and characteristic vegetation.
- Do not bind the entire Week 7 biome lesson to Week 6 coverage.
- Do not rewrite the original Week 6 definition.

## Evidence
- School source: *2ND TERM S1 BIOLOGY.doc*, Week 6 content list and block 527; Week 7 scheme and teaching blocks beginning 573.
- FAO, *Country Report on the State of Plant Genetic Resources for Food and Agriculture: Nigeria* — Nigeria's ecological zones and forest/savanna subdivisions: https://www.fao.org/4/i1500e/Nigeria.pdf

The scope finding is based on the internal placement and depth of the two school-source weeks; no source content has been changed.
