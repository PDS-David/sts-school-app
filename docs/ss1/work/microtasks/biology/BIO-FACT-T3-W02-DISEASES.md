## 2. Cross-table corrections
1. **Disease versus symptom:** diarrhoea is a symptom. The row containing *Giardia* should be titled **Giardiasis**.
2. **Disease versus syndrome:** pneumonia has multiple causes; it should not be assigned one organism in all cases.
3. **Pathogen versus vector/carrier:** *Vibrio cholerae*, *Salmonella Typhi* and *Plasmodium* are pathogens/parasites. Mosquitoes and tsetse flies are vectors. Houseflies can mechanically carry contaminants but are not the biological vector for cholera or typhoid.
4. **Airborne versus contact transmission:** measles, TB and chickenpox have airborne transmission components; the table's repeated “contact” label is inadequate.
5. **Prevention versus treatment:** “use appropriate drugs” is not a prevention measure. Vaccination, sanitation, safe food/water, vector control and infection-prevention practices should be listed where appropriate.
6. **Eradicated disease:** smallpox is globally eradicated; the source must not imply routine ongoing community transmission.
7. **No blanket isolation advice:** isolation requirements depend on the disease, infectious period and public-health guidance. Do not teach blanket isolation as the response to every infection.
8. **No diagnosis from symptoms:** many symptoms overlap between diseases. Student tables should say “common features may include” and avoid definitive diagnosis from symptoms alone.
