# Commercial branding and source-object disposition

Inspected rendered source pages 1–3 from the original First, Second and Third Term Further Mathematics documents. The archive member hashes were checked before review.

- **First Term:** page 1 contains a full-page commercial tutor promotional/vision poster. Page 2 contains a tutor logo/letterhead above the subject heading. The running footer identifies the tutor and term on subsequent pages.
- **Second Term:** the first page contains the tutor letterhead above the subject heading and the scheme of work; the same letterhead and tutor-name running footer recur on subsequent pages.
- **Third Term:** page 1 contains the same commercial promotional poster and letterhead; the tutor-name header/footer continues in the rendered document.

These objects are source branding/advertising, not mathematical teaching material. The lesson package does not reproduce the commercial tutor's contact details or promotional copy. No phone number, email address or sales contact was copied into the repository.

**Disposition:** source visual inspection and object classification are complete for the three terms. The materials should not be delivered with the tutor's branding/contact details unless the school confirms permission and approves a clean school-branded replacement. `FM-FACT-VENDOR-PERMISSION` therefore remains BLOCKED pending owner/licence decision. This review does not claim app rendering or final document layout verification.
