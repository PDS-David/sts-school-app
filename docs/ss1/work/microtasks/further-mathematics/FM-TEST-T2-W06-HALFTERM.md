## Section B — Structured questions (30 marks)

### 1. Arithmetic progression (5 marks)

An AP has fifth term 14 and tenth term 29. Find (a) the first term, (b) the common difference, and (c) the twentieth term.

**Marking guide:**  
a+4d=14 and a+9d=29 (1); 5d=15 so d=3 (1); a=2 (1); T20=2+19(3)=59 (2).

### 2. Geometric progression (5 marks)

Find the sum of the first five terms of 2,−4,8,−16,...

**Marking guide:**  
a=2,r=−2 (1); S5=a(1−r^5)/(1−r) (2); S5=2(1−(−2)^5)/3=22 (2).

### 3. One-variable inequalities (5 marks)

(a) Solve x²−5x+6≤0. (3 marks)  
(b) Solve |2x−1|>3. (2 marks)

**Marking guide:**  
(a) (x−2)(x−3)≤0, so 2≤x≤3 (3).  
(b) 2x−1>3 or 2x−1<−3, so x>2 or x<−1 (2).

### 4. Two-variable inequalities and optimisation (5 marks)

A feasible region is given by x+y≤4, x≥0 and y≥0. If Z=3x+2y, find the maximum value of Z and the point where it occurs.

**Marking guide:**  
Vertices (0,0),(4,0),(0,4) (2); Z values 0,12,8 (2); maximum 12 at (4,0) (1).

### 5. Function parameters and evaluation (5 marks)

Let f(x)=ax+b, where f(2)=5 and f(3)=8. Find a,b and f(−2).

**Marking guide:**  
2a+b=5 and 3a+b=8 (1); a=3 (1); b=−1 (1); f(−2)=−7 (2).

### 6. Mapping and function types (5 marks)

Let A={1,2,3}, B={a,b,c,d}, with 1→a, 2→b and 3→c. State the domain, codomain, range, and whether the mapping is one-to-one and onto.

**Marking guide:**  
Domain A (1); codomain B (1); range {a,b,c} (1); one-to-one (1); into/not onto because d is not reached (1).
