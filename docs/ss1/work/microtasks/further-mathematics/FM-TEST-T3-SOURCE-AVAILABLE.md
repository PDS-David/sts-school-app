## Section B — Structured questions (40 marks)

### 1. Flowcharts and tracing (6 marks)

(a) Draw a flowchart to input a number n and output “EVEN” if n is divisible by 2, otherwise output “ODD”. (4 marks)  
(b) Trace it for n=15. (2 marks)

**Marking guide:** START/STOP (1); input parallelogram (1); correct decision n mod 2=0 and labelled branches (2); output ODD for 15 (2).

### 2. Gradients and equations (7 marks)

(a) Find the gradient of the line through A(−2,3) and B(4,−9). (2 marks)  
(b) Find its equation. (3 marks)  
(c) State its y-intercept. (2 marks)

**Marking guide:** m=(−9−3)/(4−(−2))=−12/6=−2 (2); y−3=−2(x+2), so y=−2x−1 (3); y-intercept −1 (2).

### 3. Angle of slope and line relationships (7 marks)

(a) Find the angle of inclination of a line with gradient −1. (2 marks)  
(b) Determine whether y=3x+2 and 6x−2y+5=0 are parallel. (2 marks)  
(c) Find the gradient of a line perpendicular to 4x+2y−7=0. (3 marks)

**Marking guide:** inclination 135° (2); second equation y=3x+5/2, equal gradients 3, hence parallel (2); original gradient −2, perpendicular gradient 1/2 (3).

### 4. Magnitude and unit vectors (7 marks)

Let r=−6i+8j. Find (a) |r|, (b) the unit vector in its direction, and (c) verify the unit vector has magnitude 1.

**Marking guide:** |r|=√(36+64)=10 (2); unit vector −3/5 i+4/5 j (3); √(9/25+16/25)=1 (2).

### 5. Vector operations and direction cosines (7 marks)

Given a=2i−j and b=−i+3j, find (a) 2a−3b, (b) its magnitude, and (c) its direction cosines.

**Marking guide:** 2a=4i−2j; 3b=−3i+9j (2); 2a−3b=7i−11j (2); magnitude √170 (1); direction cosines 7/√170 and −11/√170 (2).

### 6. Angle between lines and vertical lines (6 marks)

(a) Find the acute angle between y=2x+1 and y=−x+4. (3 marks)  
(b) Find the equation of the line through (5,−2) and (5,7). Explain why the usual gradient formula cannot be used. (3 marks)

**Marking guide:** gradients 2 and −1; tan α=3; α≈71.57° (3); x-coordinates equal so vertical line x=5; Δx=0 gives undefined gradient (3).
