Status: **IMPLEMENTED_TESTING_DEFERRED**. Lesson object: `SUP-SS1-PHYSICS-T1-W01-LABORATORY-FAMILIARISATION`.

The original Week One scheme includes laboratory familiarisation, but the hash-verified original First Term .doc does not teach it. A bounded independent supplement has therefore been added to `physics.json`.

It covers:
- the purpose of the Physics laboratory;
- common measurement apparatus and their uses;
- safe working habits and hazard reporting;
- basic measurement routine and recording units;
- emergency response boundaries.

It does **not** assume that the school has a specific burner, eyewash, fire blanket or other facility. Students are instructed to ask the teacher where available equipment is located. It does not authorise unsupervised experiments or handling of hazardous apparatus.
