## Important corrections in the expanded lesson

- The source's short formula note does not clearly distinguish a single liquid surface from a two-sided soap film. The lesson states `γ = F/L` for one surface and `F = 2γL` for a film with two surfaces.
- Ship flotation is buoyancy, not surface tension.
- Oil rising in a lamp wick and liquid rise in a narrow tube are primarily capillarity applications; they are treated separately in Week Eight.
- Suitable surfactants lower water's surface tension; not every impurity has the same effect.
