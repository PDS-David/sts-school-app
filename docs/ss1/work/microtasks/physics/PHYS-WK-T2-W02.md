## Corrections

- “Resistant thermometer” corrected to **resistance thermometer**.
- Mercury freezing point corrected to approximately **−38.8 °C**, not +39 °C (NIST).
- Clinical thermometer range is described as a narrow range around body temperature; the source's broad 35–45 °C statement is not repeated.
- Water's anomalous expansion and wetting of glass are included.
- Evaporation is surface-only and can occur below the boiling point; boiling occurs throughout the liquid at its boiling point for the prevailing pressure.
- The source's boiling MCQ is ambiguous between heating rate and boiling point. It remains a non-blocking open item; Q13 provides a clearer replacement question.
