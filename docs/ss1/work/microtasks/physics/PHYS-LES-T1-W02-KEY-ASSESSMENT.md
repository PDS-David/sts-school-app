### EVALUATION — page 7 (7 marks)

1. Define:
   - **Length:** distance between two points (1 mark).
   - **Time interval:** duration between two events (1 mark).
2. List five suitable instruments for measuring length (5 marks): metre rule, tape rule, vernier callipers, micrometer screw gauge, and callipers (external/internal as appropriate). Accept other suitable calibrated length instruments with correct use.

### EVALUATION — page 9 (5 marks)

1. Differentiate mass and weight (3 marks):
   - Mass is the amount of matter/inertia of a body; weight is the gravitational force on it.
   - Mass is scalar and is measured in kilograms; weight is vector and is measured in newtons.
   - Mass is effectively constant for a body in ordinary contexts; weight changes with gravitational field strength.
2. Weight of 5000 g at `g=10 m s⁻²` (2 marks):
   - `m = 5000 g = 5 kg` (1)
   - `W = mg = 5 × 10 = 50 N` (1)
