# SS1 Bookkeeping — First Term Examination

**Class:** SS1 | **Term:** First Term | **Scheme slot:** Week 12 examination  
**Duration:** 1 hour 30 minutes | **Total:** 60 marks  
**Instructions:** Answer all questions. Show workings. Use ₦ for all monetary amounts.  
**Provenance:** Project-authored examination paper and marking guide; not recovered from the original source DOCX.

## Section A — Objective test (10 marks)

Choose one answer. Each question carries 1 mark.

1. Bookkeeping is the systematic recording of:
   A. business financial transactions  B. staff attendance  C. advertisements  D. weather reports
2. The cash book records:
   A. only credit sales  B. cash and bank transactions  C. only assets  D. only purchases
3. Cash received is entered on the:
   A. debit side  B. credit side  C. balance sheet  D. journal narration
4. A cheque payment is entered in the:
   A. bank debit column  B. bank credit column  C. cash debit column  D. sales column
5. A contra entry is a transfer between:
   A. sales and purchases  B. cash and bank  C. capital and drawings  D. debtors and creditors
6. The double-entry rule requires:
   A. equal debit and credit amounts  B. one entry only  C. three entries always  D. no ledger
7. An increase in an asset is:
   A. credited  B. debited  C. ignored  D. written as income
8. Posting transfers entries to:
   A. ledger accounts  B. invoices  C. receipts only  D. stock cards
9. A balance b/d is:
   A. brought down from the previous period  B. carried down at period end  C. a discount  D. an invoice number
10. The letter C in a folio column commonly identifies:
    A. cash sale  B. contra entry  C. capital  D. creditor

## Section B — Short-answer questions (10 marks)

## Section C — Practical questions (40 marks)

16. **Cash book (15 marks).** On 1 April, the business of Kola Stores had cash ₦25,000 and bank ₦80,000. The following transactions occurred:
    - Apr 2: cash sales ₦18,000
    - Apr 4: paid shop rent in cash ₦7,000
    - Apr 6: deposited ₦12,000 cash into the bank
    - Apr 9: received a cheque from a customer ₦20,000 and banked it immediately
    - Apr 12: paid a supplier by cheque ₦16,000
    - Apr 15: withdrew ₦5,000 from bank for office cash

    Prepare a two-column cash book extract and balance both columns.

17. **Double entry and ledger posting (15 marks).** Record the following transactions in journal form and identify the ledger account to be debited and credited:
    - Owner introduced cash ₦120,000.
    - Bought office equipment for cash ₦30,000.
    - Bought goods on credit from Musa ₦22,000.
    - Paid Musa ₦10,000 by bank transfer.
    - Received ₦8,000 cash from a debtor.

18. **Cash book and errors (10 marks).**
    a. State two errors that can occur when recording cash book transactions. (2)
    b. Explain why a cash-to-bank transfer is not treated as income or an expense. (2)
    c. A cash book has debit total ₦74,000 and credit total ₦59,000. Calculate the balance c/d and state the side on which it is entered. (3)
    d. State the side on which the balance b/d will appear in the next period. (3)

**Section B:**

**Question 16 (15 marks):**
- Correct opening balances and receipts: cash opening 25,000; bank opening 80,000; cash sales 18,000; bank cheque receipt 20,000 (4).
- Correct payments: rent cash 7,000; supplier cheque 16,000 (2).
- Correct contra entries on both columns: deposit 12,000 (Dr Bank / Cr Cash) and bank withdrawal 5,000 (Dr Cash / Cr Bank) (4).
- Closing cash = 25,000 + 18,000 − 7,000 − 12,000 + 5,000 = **₦29,000** (2).
- Closing bank = 80,000 + 12,000 + 20,000 − 16,000 − 5,000 = **₦91,000** (2).
- Correct balancing presentation (1).

**Question 18 (10 marks):**
a. Any two valid errors, 1 each.
