## Coverage

Cash book; single and double columns; contra entries; double entry; debit/credit rules; ledger accounts; posting; balancing and interpretation of balances.

## Section A — Multiple choice (10 marks)

1. The cash book is both a book of original entry and a:
   A. ledger account  B. balance sheet  C. trial balance  D. invoice
2. A cash discount is allowed to a customer who:
   A. pays promptly  B. buys on credit only  C. returns goods  D. pays wages
3. A cash-to-bank deposit is recorded as:
   A. Dr Cash, Cr Bank  B. Dr Bank, Cr Cash  C. Dr Purchases, Cr Cash  D. Dr Bank, Cr Sales
4. The normal balance of a cash account is:
   A. credit  B. debit  C. nil  D. suspense
5. A ledger is used to:
   A. classify transactions by account  B. issue cheques  C. store stock physically  D. calculate tax only
6. If the debit total of an account is ₦30,000 and credit total is ₦22,000, the balance is:
   A. ₦8,000 debit  B. ₦8,000 credit  C. ₦52,000 debit  D. ₦52,000 credit
7. Drawings represent:
   A. owner withdrawals for personal use  B. business sales  C. bank loans  D. purchases returns
8. A credit entry in the bank column of the cash book may represent:
   A. money received by cheque  B. a cheque payment  C. cash sales  D. cash introduced
9. The folio column helps users:
   A. trace corresponding ledger references  B. calculate depreciation  C. record dates  D. total columns
10. Every double-entry transaction must have equal:
    A. account names  B. debit and credit amounts  C. number of accounts  D. dates

## Section C — Practical questions (20 marks)

17. **Three-account double entry (8 marks).** Record these transactions in journal form:
    - Owner started business with cash ₦100,000.
    - Bought goods for cash ₦18,000.
    - Bought furniture on credit from Ade Stores ₦25,000.
    - Paid transport in cash ₦3,000.

18. **Cash book balancing (8 marks).** A trader starts with cash ₦20,000 and bank ₦60,000. During the week: cash sales ₦14,000; paid wages cash ₦6,000; deposited cash ₦8,000 into bank; received a customer cheque ₦12,000 and banked it; paid supplier by cheque ₦15,000. Calculate closing cash and bank balances and identify the contra entry.

19. **Posting explanation (4 marks).** Explain how the purchase of furniture on credit from Ade Stores in Question 17 is posted to the ledger.

## Answers and marking guide

**Section A:** 1 A; 2 A; 3 B; 4 B; 5 A; 6 A; 7 A; 8 B; 9 A; 10 B.

**Section B:**
11. Single-column has one money column; double-column has cash and bank columns (2). Double-column records cash and bank movements separately and can show contra entries (2).
12. Transfer between cash and bank within one cash book (2); examples: cash deposited into bank; cash withdrawn from bank for office use (1 each).
13. Assets: increase Dr/decrease Cr; liabilities: increase Cr/decrease Dr; capital: increase Cr/decrease Dr; expenses: Dr; income: Cr (1 each).
14. Classifies transactions by account and provides balances/history (2); separate accounts make information easier to find and summarise (1).
15. Balance c/d is the balancing figure entered on the shorter side at period end; balance b/d is brought into the next period on the opposite side (1 each).
16. Any two: arithmetic, omitted transaction, wrong column, wrong side, duplicate entry, incorrect contra treatment (1 each).
