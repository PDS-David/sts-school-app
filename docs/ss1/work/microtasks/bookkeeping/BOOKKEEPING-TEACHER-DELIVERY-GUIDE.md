# SS1 Bookkeeping — teacher delivery guide

**Package:** `backend/src/db/ss1/subjects/bookkeeping.json`  
**Class:** SS 1  
**Schedule:** Three terms, twelve scheduled weeks per term  
**Status:** 32 approved project/original-source lessons; revision and examination resources indexed separately.

## How to use this guide

1. Teach the lessons in the order shown. The lesson IDs are the stable package identifiers.
2. Start each lesson with its stated objectives. Teach the body and worked examples before assigning the practice.
3. Let students attempt practice before discussing the answer. Use the lesson's marking criteria, not just the final answer.
4. Where a topic includes a cash book, ledger, trial balance, trading account or balance sheet, require students to draw the format and show the postings/calculations.
5. Use the separate Week 11 revision resource for retrieval practice and the Week 12 examination resource for summative assessment.

The package's practice answers and marking allocations are part of the teacher-facing source data. Do not expose answer keys to students before an attempt if the platform supports separate teacher/student visibility.
