# SS1 Bookkeeping — Second Term Examination

**Class:** SS1 | **Term:** Second Term | **Scheme slot:** Week 12 examination  
**Duration:** 1 hour 30 minutes | **Total:** 60 marks  
**Instructions:** Answer all questions. Show workings. Use ₦ for monetary amounts.  
**Provenance:** Project-authored examination based on the provisional second-term Bookkeeping reconstruction. It is not recovered from the duplicate second-term source file.

## Section A — Objective test (10 marks)

1. The sales day book records:
   A. credit sales of goods for resale  B. cash sales  C. sale of equipment  D. wages
2. Goods returned by a credit customer are entered in:
   A. returns inwards book  B. returns outwards book  C. purchases day book  D. cash book
3. A general journal entry must have:
   A. equal debit and credit amounts  B. only a debit  C. no narration  D. no date
4. A creditor is a person or business to whom the business:
   A. owes money  B. lends money  C. sells only for cash  D. pays wages
5. Trade discount is deducted from:
   A. list price  B. bank balance  C. capital  D. gross profit
6. Discount received is normally:
   A. income  B. expense  C. asset  D. drawings
7. The discount allowed column of a three-column cash book is on the:
   A. debit side  B. credit side  C. both sides only for contra  D. balance sheet
8. A cheque issued but not yet presented is:
   A. an unpresented cheque  B. an uncredited deposit  C. a dishonoured cheque  D. a contra entry
9. A bank reconciliation statement compares:
   A. cash-book and bank-statement bank balances  B. sales and purchases  C. assets and liabilities  D. capital and drawings
10. The ledger is used to:
    A. classify transactions into accounts  B. replace source documents  C. store inventory  D. prepare invoices only

## Section B — Short answers (10 marks)

11. Define books of original entry and state one reason for using them. (2)
12. Distinguish returns inwards from returns outwards. (2)
13. Distinguish trade discount from cash discount. (2)
14. State one debit and one credit entry associated with discount allowed/received. (2)
15. State two timing differences that may appear in a bank reconciliation. (2)

## Section C — Practical questions (40 marks)

16. **Day books and journal (10 marks).** For each transaction, name the appropriate book of original entry:
    a. Credit sale of goods ₦40,000.
    b. Credit purchase of goods ₦25,000.
    c. Customer returns goods ₦3,000.
    d. Return goods to supplier ₦2,000.
    e. Buy office equipment on credit ₦60,000.
    f. Cash sales ₦12,000.
    g. Correct an error of posting through a journal.
    h. Credit sale of goods ₦18,000.
    i. Cash purchase of goods ₦7,000.
    j. Credit purchase of goods ₦11,000.

17. **Trade and cash discount (8 marks).** A supplier invoices goods at ₦120,000 less trade discount of 15%. The buyer pays the net amount promptly and receives a cash discount of 2%. Calculate the trade discount, net invoice amount, cash discount and amount paid.

18. **Three-column cash book (10 marks).** Record the following:
    - Received a cheque from Ada ₦30,000 and allowed her ₦1,500 discount.
    - Paid Kemi by cheque ₦24,000 and received ₦1,000 discount.
    State the cash-book entries and the corresponding personal ledger settlement amounts.

19. **Bank reconciliation (12 marks).** The cash book shows a favourable bank balance of ₦52,000. Cheques issued but not yet presented are ₦11,000. Deposits not yet credited are ₦7,000. A bank charge of ₦500 has not yet been entered in the cash book.
    a. Update the cash-book balance for the bank charge. (3)
    b. Starting with the adjusted cash-book balance, calculate the bank-statement balance using the two timing differences. (5)
    c. Explain why the unpresented cheque and uncredited deposit do not automatically mean that the cash book is wrong. (4)

## Marking guide

**Section A:** 1 A; 2 A; 3 A; 4 A; 5 A; 6 A; 7 A; 8 A; 9 A; 10 A.

**Section B:** Award 1 mark per valid point. Books of original entry are chronological first records and support ledger posting; returns inwards are customer returns and returns outwards are returns to suppliers; trade discount is deducted from list price while cash discount rewards prompt payment; discount allowed is debited as an expense while discount received is credited as income; timing differences include unpresented cheques and deposits not yet credited.

**Question 16 (10 marks):** a Sales day book; b Purchases day book; c Returns inwards book; d Returns outwards book; e General journal; f Cash book; g General journal; h Sales day book; i Cash book; j Purchases day book. Award 1 each.

**Question 17 (8 marks):** Trade discount = ₦18,000 (2); net invoice = ₦102,000 (2); cash discount = 2% × ₦102,000 = ₦2,040 (2); amount paid = **₦99,960** (2).

**Question 18 (10 marks):**
- Ada: Bank debit ₦30,000; discount allowed debit-column discount ₦1,500; Ada's personal account is credited/settled ₦31,500 (5).
- Kemi: Bank credit ₦24,000; discount received credit-column discount ₦1,000; Kemi's personal account is debited/settled ₦25,000 (5).

**Question 19 (12 marks):** Adjusted cash-book balance = ₦52,000 − ₦500 = ₦51,500 (3). Bank-statement balance = ₦51,500 + ₦11,000 − ₦7,000 = **₦55,500** (5). The two items are timing differences: one has been recorded by the business but not yet processed by the bank, and the other has been recorded by the bank but not yet credited (4).

**Examiner check:** Q17 payment ₦99,960; Q19 adjusted cash-book ₦51,500 and statement ₦55,500.
