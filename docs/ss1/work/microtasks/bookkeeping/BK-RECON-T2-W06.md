# SS1 Bookkeeping — Reconstructed Second Term, Week 6

**Project-authored reconstruction — Bookkeeping; not recovered source material**

## Topic
Posting from subsidiary books to ledger accounts

## Learning outcomes
Learners should be able to explain posting; post individual customer and supplier entries; post day-book totals to the appropriate accounts; and use dates, particulars and folio references correctly.

## Prerequisite
Weeks 1–5: books of original entry, sales/purchases/returns books, ledger classes and the basic double-entry rules taught in the first term.

## 1. Meaning of posting
Posting is the transfer of information from a book of original entry to the appropriate ledger accounts. It allows the business to collect all entries affecting one account in one place.

A ledger account is often shown in a T-account format:
- debit entries appear on the left;
- credit entries appear on the right.

The particulars column names the corresponding account. The folio column provides a cross-reference to the originating book or ledger page.

## 2. Posting the sales day book
Suppose the sales day book contains:
- 2 October: Ayo Stores, 10,000;
- 5 October: Bisi Mart, 6,000;
- total: 16,000.

The posting is:
- debit Ayo Stores 10,000;
- debit Bisi Mart 6,000;
- credit Sales Account 16,000.

| Ayo Stores Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Sales | 10,000 | |

| Bisi Mart Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Sales | 6,000 | |

| Sales Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Ayo Stores and Bisi Mart — total sales | | 16,000 |

The customer accounts are debited individually because each customer owes the business. The Sales Account is credited with the total.

## 3. Posting the purchases day book
Suppose the purchases day book contains:
- 3 October: Chuma Traders, 8,000;
- 7 October: Dera Supplies, 5,000;
- total: 13,000.

The posting is:
- debit Purchases Account 13,000;
- credit Chuma Traders 8,000;
- credit Dera Supplies 5,000.

| Purchases Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Total purchases | 13,000 | |

| Chuma Traders Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Purchases | | 8,000 |

| Dera Supplies Account | Debit | Credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| Purchases | | 5,000 |

Each supplier is credited because the business owes that supplier.

## 4. Posting returns books
For returns inwards:
- debit Returns Inwards Account with the total;
- credit each customer's personal account with the amount returned.

For returns outwards:
- credit Returns Outwards Account with the total;
- debit each supplier's personal account with the amount returned.

Example: customer Eni returns goods worth 1,200. Credit Eni's account 1,200 and debit Returns Inwards 1,200.

## 5. Posting procedure
1. Check the date and source document.
2. Identify whether the entry is an individual personal-account posting or a period total.
3. Identify the correct debit and credit accounts.
4. Enter the amount on the correct side.
5. Enter the corresponding account name in particulars.
6. Add the folio reference when the ledger page is known.
7. Check that the total debit equals the total credit for the transaction class.

## 6. Guided practice
A sales day book has credit sales of 7,500 to Fola Stores and 4,500 to Goke Mart. State the postings.

**Answer:** Debit Fola Stores 7,500; debit Goke Mart 4,500; credit Sales Account 12,000.

A purchases day book has credit purchases of 9,000 from Hadi Traders and 3,000 from Ire Supplies. State the postings.

**Answer:** Debit Purchases Account 12,000; credit Hadi Traders 9,000; credit Ire Supplies 3,000.

## 7. Independent assessment
**Q1 (3 marks).** What is posting?
**Answer:** Transferring entries from books of original entry to the appropriate ledger accounts.
**Marking:** 3 marks.

**Q2 (5 marks).** A sales day book records credit sales of 14,000 to Jide Stores and 6,000 to Kola Mart. Show the ledger postings.
**Answer:** Debit Jide Stores 14,000; debit Kola Mart 6,000; credit Sales Account 20,000.
**Marking:** Each customer debit, 1.5 marks; Sales credit and total, 2 marks.

**Q3 (5 marks).** A purchases day book records 11,000 from Lami Traders and 9,000 from Musa Wholesalers. Show the postings.
**Answer:** Debit Purchases Account 20,000; credit Lami Traders 11,000; credit Musa Wholesalers 9,000.
**Marking:** Purchases debit, 2; supplier credits, 1 each; amounts/total, 1.

**Q4 (5 marks).** Goods worth 2,400 are returned by a credit customer, Nene Stores. State both ledger entries and explain the effect on the customer's account.
**Answer:** Debit Returns Inwards 2,400; credit Nene Stores 2,400. The customer's debt to the business decreases by 2,400.
**Marking:** Correct debit, 2; correct credit, 2; explanation, 1.

**Total: 18 marks.**
