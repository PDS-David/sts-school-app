# SS1 Bookkeeping — First Term, Week 4 Supplement

**Project-authored replacement practice — Bookkeeping; not a correction of the defective source figures**

## Topic
Posting from a double-column cash book to ledger accounts: a complete worked exercise

## Learning outcomes
Learners should be able to enter cash and bank transactions in a double-column cash book; balance the cash and bank columns separately; post the non-contra entries to the corresponding ledger accounts; and explain why contra entries are not posted a second time.

## Prerequisite
Learners should understand the double-column cash book, contra entries and the debit-and-credit rules taught in the earlier weeks.

## 1. Posting rule
The cash book is both a book of original entry and the cash/bank ledger account. The other accounts are completed by posting the opposite entry:
- a cash-book receipt is on the debit side of the cash book; the corresponding customer, income or capital account is credited;
- a cash-book payment is on the credit side; the corresponding expense, asset, purchase or supplier account is debited;
- a contra entry affects the cash and bank columns of the same cash book. Mark C on both sides and do not post it again to separate cash and bank ledger accounts.

A ledger account has Date, Particulars, Folio and Amount columns on each side. Use the account name as the corresponding particulars and enter the assigned folio reference.

## 2. Complete independent practice question
A business has opening balances on 1 June of cash 6,000 and bank 15,000. The following transactions occur:

| Jun. 5 | Bello, a supplier, paid by cheque | 2,000 |
| Jun. 6 | Cash purchases | 1,200 |
| Jun. 7 | Cash received from Chidi, a debtor | 1,800 |
| Jun. 8 | Wages paid in cash | 650 |
| Jun. 9 | Electricity paid by bank transfer | 450 |
| Jun. 10 | Cash deposited into the bank | 2,000 |
| Jun. 11 | Cash withdrawn from bank for office use | 500 |
| Jun. 12 | Stationery paid in cash | 250 |
| Jun. 13 | Commission received directly into bank | 900 |
| Jun. 14 | Insurance paid by cheque | 600 |
| Jun. 15 | Cheque received from Efe, a debtor, and banked | 1,400 |

| **Totals** | | **13,450** | **22,800** | **Totals** | | **13,450** | **22,800** |

Checks:
- Cash debit total = 6,000 + 2,400 + 1,800 + 500 + 1,750 + 1,000 = 13,450.
- Cash payments before balance = 800 + 1,200 + 650 + 2,000 + 250 + 300 = 5,200; closing cash = 13,450 − 5,200 = **8,250**.
- Bank debit total = 15,000 + 3,500 + 2,000 + 900 + 1,400 = 22,800.
- Bank payments before balance = 2,000 + 450 + 500 + 600 + 1,100 + 120 + 2,500 = 7,270; closing bank = 22,800 − 7,270 = **15,530**.
- Contra entries: 2,000 deposited and 500 withdrawn. Each appears on both sides of the cash book and is marked C.

Bring down cash 8,250 and bank 15,530 on the debit side at the start of the next period.

Post all non-contra entries as follows:

The cash and bank balances remain in the cash book, which itself serves as those ledger accounts. The 2,000 deposit and 500 withdrawal are contra entries and are not posted again to separate cash/bank accounts.

## 5. Independent assessment

**Q1 (5 marks).** Calculate the closing cash and bank balances from the worked cash book.
**Answer:** Cash 8,250 debit; Bank 15,530 debit.
**Marking:** Cash calculation 2; cash answer 1; bank calculation 1; bank answer 1.

**Q2 (5 marks).** State the ledger entries for the cheque received from Ada (3,500) and the cheque payment to Bello (2,000).
**Answer:** Ada: debit Bank 3,500 in the cash book and credit Ada's personal account 3,500. Bello: credit Bank 2,000 in the cash book and debit Bello's personal account 2,000.
**Marking:** Ada cash-book/bank debit 1; Ada credit 1; Bello bank credit 1; Bello debit 1; amounts correct 1.

**Q3 (4 marks).** Why are the 2,000 cash deposit and 500 bank withdrawal not posted again to separate cash and bank ledger accounts?
**Answer:** Each is a contra entry between the business's own cash and bank balances. Both sides are already recorded in the cash book and marked C, so a second ledger posting would duplicate the transfer.
**Marking:** Identifies transfer between own balances 2; both sides in same cash book/C 1; avoids duplicate posting 1.

**Q4 (4 marks).** Name four non-contra transactions from the question and the ledger accounts affected.
**Answer:** Any four correct pairs, e.g. rent: Rent Expense; furniture: Furniture; commission received: Commission Received; cash sales: Sales Account.
**Marking:** 1 mark per correct transaction/account pair.
