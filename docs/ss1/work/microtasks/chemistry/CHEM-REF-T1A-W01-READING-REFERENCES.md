## 1. Ababio reference

**Author identity:** Corroborated. The abbreviation “O.Y Ababio” and the fuller name “Osei Yaw A.” in the source's reference list point to Osei Yaw Ababio.

**Title and edition evidence:**
- The Ladoke Akintola University of Technology, Olusegun Oke Library catalogue records *New School Chemistry for Senior Secondary Schools*, by Osei Yaw Ababio, Onitsha: Africana First Publishers PLC, 2013. This is a library catalogue record and supports a real title/author/publisher/year variant.
- Google Books records an earlier *New School Chemistry* by Osei Yaw Ababio, Africana-FEP, 1985.
- A public Chemistry recommended-text list gives *New School Chemistry for Senior Secondary Schools*, fourth edition (2009), Africana FIRST Publishers Limited, Onitsha. This is a public examination-reference list, not the publisher's own catalogue.
- A separate 2016 school-text list identifies a 2016 edition and names L.E.S. Akpanisi and Herbert Igwe as revisers. These records demonstrate that more than one edition/title variant exists.

**Conclusion:** The author identity is supported. The source's short title is consistent with a known Ababio book, but the exact edition used by the curriculum author is **UNRESOLVED**. ## 2. Ojokuku reference

**Spelling:** “Collegs” in the Week One reading assignment is a typographical error. The opening reference list spells “Colleges”, which supplies the intended spelling. This corrects the typo only; it does not establish the exact book title.

**Title identity evidence:**
- The University of Ibadan International School booklist (2019/20) identifies *Understanding Chemistry for Schools and Colleges* by Godwin O. Ojokuku, Press-On Chem Resources. This is a different title from the source's “Practical Chemistry for Schools and Colleges”.
- A public school textbook list identifies a separate *Practical Chemistry* by Godwin Ojokuku, Press-On Publishers, but does not give enough bibliographic detail to establish that it is the source's intended title.
- A secondary scholarly bibliography cites G.O. Ojokuku (2012), *Text book of practical chemistry for schools and colleges*, fourth edition, Zaria: Mac Multimedia Designers. This is a secondary citation, not a publisher or library catalogue record; it does not prove that the curriculum's abbreviated reference is to this exact edition.
- Search of public library/publisher catalogue evidence did not locate a primary record that resolves the exact title, edition and publisher for the source's “Practical Chemistry for Schools and Colleges”.

**Conclusion:** The typo “Collegs” is resolved to “Colleges”, but the exact Ojokuku title is **AMBIGUOUS**. It may refer to a separate practical chemistry book; it must not be silently replaced with *Understanding Chemistry for Schools and Colleges*. The assignment's pages 2–15 are **NOT VERIFIED** because the exact edition was not supplied or inspected.
