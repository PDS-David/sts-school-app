## Findings
1. **Apparatus list punctuation:** line 269 joins adjacent names without reliable separators (including “funnel (plastic or glass) thistle funnel” and “Woulff’s bottle tripod stand”). These must remain distinct items; punctuation/formatting must be checked against the original document layout before any later transcription.
2. **Desiccator:** the standard apparatus spelling is “desiccator”, not “DESSICATOR”; “desiccated” describes the state of being dried and is not the apparatus name. The source's line 309 wording is ambiguous and should not be silently normalised.
3. **Wire gauze:** the standard term is “wire gauze”, not “WIRE GUAZE”. It is a mesh support used to support glassware during heating and distribute heat; it is not a vessel.
4. **Woulff/Woulfe bottle:** both spellings appear in historical technical sources. The Smithsonian National Museum of American History describes a Woulff bottle as a two- or three-outlet vessel. The Science Museum Group records the variant “Woulfe bottle” and describes its use in gas/liquid apparatus. Therefore the source's “Woulff’s bottle” is not inherently an error; retain its original form.
5. **Reflux condenser:** “reflux condenser” is the standard equipment term; a condenser cools vapour and returns condensed liquid to a reaction vessel in reflux. The source's hyphenation is a style choice, not a different apparatus. No apparatus set-up or operating instruction is added.
6. **Descriptions:** the source's material/shape/construction descriptions at lines 279–325 should be checked against the exact apparatus item and its drawing, not treated as universal across all models. The available inventory does not provide sufficient image/paragraph alignment to validate every descriptive adjective. This remains a precision limit, not a reason to invent dimensions or construction.
7. **Typos and bibliography:** “DEFINTION”, “WIRE GUAZE”, the stray apostrophe at line 323 and “Collegs” in the reading assignment are source typographical defects. The Ababio title/author details should be matched to the exact edition in the assigned reference list; no edition or page is inferred here.
