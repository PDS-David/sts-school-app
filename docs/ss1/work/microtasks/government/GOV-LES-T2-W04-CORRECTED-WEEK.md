## Teaching completed

It distinguishes pre-party resistance from modern organised nationalism; separates WWI/postwar hardship from the Great Depression beginning in 1929; explains economic exploitation, cost-of-living pressures, newspapers, parties, labour organisations and Pan-Africanism; and corrects party and newspaper dates.

Leader corrections include:
- Macaulay: NNDP 1923; Lagos Daily News founded 1925 and acquired by Macaulay/Caulcrick in 1927.
- Azikiwe: born 16 November 1904; West African Pilot 1937; NCNC 1944; Secretary-General 1946; Eastern Region Premier 1954–1959; Governor-General 1960–1963; President 1963–1966.
- Ahmadu Bello: born 1910, not 1909; NPC political-party emergence in 1951.
- Awolowo: born 1909; Action Group 1951; Western Region Premier 1954–1959; free primary education programme 1955; unsupported TUC co-founder claim not repeated.

Nana Olomu is identified and his 1894 capture/exile is noted. Jaja and Ovonramwen are cross-referenced to Week Two. Awujale is a title, and the source does not identify the officeholder/event; no name is invented. W.E.B. Du Bois is classified as a Pan-Africanist intellectual/organiser. Jimi Solanke is not presented as a political party leader. Unsupported rapid-growth and ambiguous “sold West Africa” claims are removed from the replacement.
