## Implemented teaching
The replacement recognises pre-existing African, Islamic, indigenous and mission educational traditions; gives the correct University College Ibadan chronology (1948; independent in 1962); rejects the paternalistic claim that West Africans were unready for the world economy; explains precolonial trade and diplomacy; qualifies cultural, rural/urban and economic impacts; distinguishes indirect rule, direct administration, assimilation and association; and corrects the six-row comparison conceptually.

For Governor powers, the lesson uses Nigeria-specific examples only: the Nigeria (Legislative Council) Order in Council 1922 and the Nigeria (Constitution) Order in Council 1951, including sections 86–87. It does not attribute unverified emergency or mercy powers to every British West African Governor.
