## Teaching completed
The replacement explains the 1989 Constitution's presidential system, two-party transition framework, recall mechanism, Abuja FCT status, rights and judiciary. It distinguishes promulgation and transition arrangements from full stable civilian operation: the Third Republic transition collapsed in 1993 after the annulment of the 12 June election. Candidate qualifications and local-government recognition are presented as possible institutional safeguards/opportunities, not guarantees of competence or development.

For 1999, it identifies Decree No. 24 of 1999, promulgated 5 May and effective 29 May 1999. The Decree uses the 1979 Constitution with amendments as its basis; it did not retain both 1979 and 1989. It distinguishes the 1995 Abacha-era draft from the 1999 Abdulsalami promulgation; gives 768 State LGAs plus six FCT Area Councils; describes federal and state courts; and supports federal-power/amendment critiques through the Exclusive and Concurrent Legislative Lists and section 9.
