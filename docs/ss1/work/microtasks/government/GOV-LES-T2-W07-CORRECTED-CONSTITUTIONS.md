## Teaching completed
The replacement corrects the 1960 constitutional monarchy, using the title Queen of Nigeria and distinguishing the monarch, Governor-General and Prime Minister. It explains the 44-member Senate under section 37: 12 Senators per Region selected by a joint sitting of regional legislative houses from Governor-nominated candidates; four Federal Territory Senators; and four selected by the Governor-General on Prime Minister's advice. It corrects regional arrangements: North and West bicameral; East unicameral.

It explains the actual federal Judicial Service Commission composition under section 120, appointment procedures under section 105 and continued Privy Council appeals under section 114. It then teaches the 1963 Republic, joint-parliamentary presidential election, Prime Minister as head of government, Supreme Court as final appellate court, constitutional supremacy under section 1, Mid-Western Region inauguration on 9 August 1963, and rights subject to constitutional limitations. Carpet-crossing is treated as a political practice, not an express constitutional permission or guaranteed reward.
