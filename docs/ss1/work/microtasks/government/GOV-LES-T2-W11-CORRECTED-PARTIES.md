## Teaching completed
The replacement teaches the four parties distinctly:
- **NNDP:** founded 1923 under Herbert Macaulay; Lagos-based; won the three Lagos elected Legislative Council seats in 1923, 1928 and 1933. National aspirations are distinguished from its actual Lagos electoral base.
- **NYM:** Lagos Youth Movement emerged 1933/34 and became the Nigerian Youth Movement in 1936; won the Lagos Town Council and three Lagos elective seats in 1938; associated with the Daily Service; the 1941 Ikoli–Akinsanya crisis weakened the movement. Nationalism predated it.
- **Action Group:** founded March 1951 under Awolowo; objectives include mass literacy/education, Western Region elections and cooperation for self-rule; Enahoro motion 31 March 1953; free primary education programme 1955. Egbe Omo Oduduwa chronology and subjective “best party” claims are qualified.
- **NPC:** emerged from Jam'iyyar Mutanen Arewa (1948/49) and became a political party in 1951. The original source omits NPC objectives, so a separately labelled summary is added from NOUN courseware and historical research. After the 1959 election, NPC formed a coalition with NCNC; Abubakar Tafawa Balewa became Prime Minister. The federal government was not NPC alone.

The source's first NNDP officer names/titles are not independently confirmed. The phrase “complete independence within the British Empire” is contradictory and exact original NYM charter wording is not confirmed. Neither is repeated as a verified fact.
