## Teaching completed
The replacement explains the 1954 Constitution's federal division of powers, federal and regional legislatures, Speaker arrangements, regional differences in electoral methods, regionalised judiciary/public service, the Governor-General's role as chair of the Federal Executive Council and the absence of a federal Nigerian Prime Minister under this arrangement. It explains derivation as one criterion in a wider revenue-sharing history, not as the only principle.

It explains the resumed Constitutional Conference at Lancaster House, London, 29 September–27 October 1958; minority-rights concerns and Willink Commission safeguards; the decision not to create new regions before independence; and the arrangements leading to independence on 1 October 1960.
