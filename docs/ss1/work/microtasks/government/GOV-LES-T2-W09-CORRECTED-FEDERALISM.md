## Teaching completed
The replacement defines federalism and traces Nigeria's constitutional development through 1954, 1960, 1963, 1979, the interrupted 1989 transition and the current amended 1999 Constitution. It distinguishes the federal bicameral National Assembly from unicameral State Houses of Assembly and notes historical regional differences. It explains the Supreme Court's final appellate role while recognising that other courts interpret and apply the Constitution.

It explains section 2(1)'s indivisible and indissoluble wording without inventing a specific criminal offence; calculates the 1999 Senate composition as three Senators per State plus one for the FCT; and distinguishes section 14's federal character principle from specific quota policies.

The reading pointer is corrected to **Chinua Achebe, The Trouble with Nigeria (1983)**. No edition/page is supplied and no book text is reproduced.
