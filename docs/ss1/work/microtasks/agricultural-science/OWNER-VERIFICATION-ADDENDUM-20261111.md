## Scope

This addendum records four targeted improvements to existing owner-approved lesson supplements. It does not claim that these additions were present in the original ZIP. The owner's confirmation that the ZIP source material used for this work is verified remains recorded separately in `OWNER-SOURCE-VERIFICATION-20261111.md`.

The following existing lesson supplements were expanded and their SHA-256 body hashes recomputed:

1. **Week 1 — Crop-science aspects:** added the commonly accepted Latin word origin of *agriculture* and distinguished etymology from the wider modern scope of the subject.
2. **Week 10 — Monocotyledonous and dicotyledonous plants:** added annual, biennial and perennial crop life cycles, with the distinction between botanical life cycle and the farmer's harvest period.
3. **Third Term Week 3 — Farm power operations:** added the meaning and scope of farm mechanization, its relationship with labour, and practical constraints. It explicitly distinguishes mechanization from farm inputs such as seed, fertilizer and pesticides.
4. **Third Term Week 6 — Embryo development and normal lamb presentation:** added approximate ewe oestrous-cycle and sheep gestation values, and the role of colostrum in passive immunity. The material is expressly non-procedural. It does not instruct learners to examine, reposition or extract a lamb, administer medication, or manage dystocia. Real animal-health concerns must be referred to a qualified veterinarian or trained livestock professional.
