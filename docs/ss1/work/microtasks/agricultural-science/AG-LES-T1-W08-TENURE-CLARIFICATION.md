The original questions use “land tenure system” for both legal arrangements and ways of acquiring plots. These are related but different concepts. The NBS GHS-Panel Wave 4 (2018/19) and Wave 5 (2023/24) surveys support family inheritance as the most commonly reported national mode of acquiring agricultural plots in those survey waves. Inheritance is not, by itself, the entire legal land-tenure system.
