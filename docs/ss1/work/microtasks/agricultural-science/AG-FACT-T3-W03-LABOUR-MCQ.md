## Distinguishing the labour concepts
1. **Labour displacement/unemployment:** the source's block 310 describes this as an adverse social consequence of mechanization. It can be a disadvantage after machinery is adopted; it is not the same thing as a constraint preventing adoption.
2. **Labour shortage/low availability:** this can constrain agricultural production and influence mechanization. FAO's 2006 *Farm Power and Mechanization for Small Farms in Sub-Saharan Africa* identifies decreasing labour availability, migration and seasonal labour-demand peaks as production constraints. It notes that a shortage of farm power seriously constrains productivity.
3. **Labour availability and cost:** FAO's 2013 *Agricultural Mechanization in Sub-Saharan Africa: Guidelines for Preparing a Strategy* states that labour availability and cost influence mechanization decisions; as labour becomes scarce and costly, the higher capital cost of machinery can become justifiable. Thus labour conditions are context-dependent: shortage can be a constraint to production, while abundant/low-cost labour can reduce the economic incentive to mechanize.

## Finding and recommended MCQ correction
- The current option D, **“Labor”**, is too vague to distinguish a shortage of available labour from the displacement of workers by machinery.
- If the intended exception is the source's own disadvantage (worker displacement), rewrite option D as **“Labour displacement/unemployment”**. The revised question could read:

> Which of the following is **NOT** a constraint to the adoption of farm mechanization in West Africa?
> A. Small farm holdings
> B. Inadequate technical know-how
> C. Lack of capital
> D. Labour displacement/unemployment

  Under that wording, **D** is the defensible exception: it is a possible consequence/disadvantage of mechanization, not a constraint to adopting it.
- If option D is intended to mean **labour shortage**, it can be a real production constraint and factor influencing mechanization; it should not be keyed as the exception.
- Recommendation: revise D to “labour displacement/unemployment” and use the revised stem above, or withdraw the unchanged MCQ until its intended meaning is clarified. No answer key is assigned to the printed wording.
