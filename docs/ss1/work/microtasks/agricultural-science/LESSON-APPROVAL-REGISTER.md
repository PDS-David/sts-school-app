## Required review protocol
For every lesson, the reviewer should:
1. Read the entire learner-facing body and check that every stated objective is actually taught at SS1 level.
2. Check factual accuracy, definitions, units, examples, safety-sensitive content and any explicit uncertainty.
3. Attempt every practice question; verify the answer and each marking criterion, including that criterion marks sum to the question total.
4. Inspect linked diagrams/tables and confirm rights, meaningful alt text, and that no unlicensed source image is reproduced.
5. Check source mapping and any delivery correction against the exact source row and hash.
6. Record one of **APPROVE**, **REVISE**, or **REJECT** for that lesson. Approval must include reviewer name, professional role/qualification, date, lesson ID and body SHA-256. A revised body requires a new hash and a fresh review.

Do not mark `verification: VERIFIED` from a content review. Verification is a separate technical/runtime stage.
