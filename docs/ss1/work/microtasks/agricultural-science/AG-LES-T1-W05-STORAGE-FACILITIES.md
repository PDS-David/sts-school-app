## Lesson body

Week 5 Assessment Supplement: Modern Agricultural Storage Facilities

An agricultural storage facility is a structure or enclosed storage system used to keep harvested produce for a period while protecting its quality and reducing losses before use, processing or sale.

## Examples of Modern Agricultural Storage Facilities

1. **Grain silo or strategic national silo:** Stores bulk dry grains such as maize, rice, wheat and sorghum. Silos are used for larger-volume or longer-term grain storage.
2. **Community warehouse:** Stores bagged grains, legumes, seeds and other durable produce until distribution or sale.
3. **Cold room:** Uses a cool, controlled environment to store perishable produce such as fruits, vegetables, dairy products, meat or fish.
4. **Improved ventilated crib:** A ventilated structure for drying and storing unthreshed grain, especially maize still on the cob.
5. **Diffuse-light store:** A storage facility used for potatoes.
6. **Improved yam barn:** Stores yams in a structure that allows the stored tubers to be monitored and helps prevent sprouting and spoilage.
7. **Cassava warehouse:** A warehouse system for storing freshly harvested cassava roots; the Nigerian Stored Products Research Institute describes a method using moist sawdust.
8. **Hermetic metal silo or drum:** A sealed storage system for grains and other dry commodities.
9. **Evaporative-cooling store:** A short-term storage structure that helps preserve fruits and vegetables where a conventional cold room is not available.

## Storage Facilities and Processing Equipment Are Different

- A **storage facility** holds produce for a period while helping to maintain its quality. Examples include a silo, warehouse, cold room, crib, yam barn and diffuse-light store.
- **Processing equipment** changes, separates or prepares produce. A miller grinds grain into flour; a sheller separates grain or kernels from cobs or pods; a dryer reduces moisture before storage. These machines may be used in the post-harvest chain, but they are not storage facilities.

The Week 5 source block 432 groups millers and shellers with storage facilities. For technical accuracy, this supplement identifies them as processing equipment and lists actual storage structures separately. The original source is not changed.
