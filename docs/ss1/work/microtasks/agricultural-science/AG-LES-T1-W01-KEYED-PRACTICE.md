## Lesson body

Week 1 Supplement: Keyed Practice and Marking Guide

This guide provides suggested answers and criterion-level marking for the Week 1 evaluation, general evaluation, weekend assignment and theory questions. Answers are based on the First Term teaching blocks and the Week 1 crop-science supplement. The Week 6 teaching blocks are used where the Week 1 questions depend on agro-based or agro-allied industries.

The source does not state a ranking for the most important reason for practising agriculture. For the weekend multiple-choice question, food is the reasoned sample choice because food is essential for human and animal life; this is a justified answer, not a ranking quoted from the source.

The Week 6 text at block 482 uses “agro-allied industries” and “agro-based industries” for industries that depend on agriculture for raw materials, and block 486 gives examples. Week 1 block 105 also uses “agro-allied” for suppliers of farm inputs. These uses are inconsistent in the source. For questions that depend on the Week 6 definition and table, this answer key follows the explicit Week 6 definition and records the inconsistency rather than silently treating the terms as universally interchangeable.

For the weekend multiple-choice item about diseases of crops and animals, the option “Pathology” is answered as written. This general term is distinct from “crop pathology,” which the Week 1 crop-science supplement defines as the study of diseases affecting crops and other plants; animal diseases belong to veterinary pathology.

## Complete answers and criterion-level marking

### P1 — Week 1 evaluation questions (18 marks)

**Question:** Answer the Week 1 evaluation questions: (a) define agriculture; (b) name the two Latin words from which the term is derived and give their meanings; (c) define agronomy, crop pathology and entomology; (d) state three functions each of veterinary medicine and agricultural extension; (e) define agro-based industries.

**Answer:** (a) Agriculture is the art and science or management of cultivating crops and rearing animals for human use, including the production and handling of plant and animal products. (b) Ager means field, farm or land; the source writes Culture in block 58 and Cultura in the weekend MCQ, both meaning cultivation. (c) Agronomy: the science of crop production and soil management. Crop pathology: the study of diseases affecting crops and other plants. Entomology: the scientific study of insects. (d) Veterinary medicine: improves the health management of farm animals; helps control pests; helps control parasites and animal diseases. Agricultural extension: trains farmers; disseminates agricultural information; links farmers with researchers. (e) Using the Week 6 definition, agro-based industries are industries that depend on agricultural raw materials to operate and produce goods.

**Marking:**
- 1 mark(s) — Defines agriculture and includes crop cultivation.
- 1 mark(s) — Includes rearing of animals and the production/handling of plant and animal products.
- 1 mark(s) — States Ager means field, farm or land.
- 1 mark(s) — States Culture/Cultura means cultivation and notes the source's spelling variation.
- 2 mark(s) — Defines agronomy as crop production and soil management.
- 2 mark(s) — Defines crop pathology as study of diseases affecting crops/plants.
- 2 mark(s) — Defines entomology as the scientific study of insects.
- 1 mark(s) — Gives veterinary health management as one function.
- 1 mark(s) — Gives pest control as a veterinary function.
- 1 mark(s) — Gives parasite and/or disease control as a veterinary function.
- 1 mark(s) — Gives farmer training as an extension function.
- 1 mark(s) — Gives dissemination of agricultural information as an extension function.
- 1 mark(s) — Gives linking farmers with researchers as an extension function.
- 2 mark(s) — Defines agro-based industries as depending on agricultural raw materials.

### P3 — General evaluation (51 marks)

**Question:** Answer the Week 1 general evaluation: (a) define agriculture; (b) list and define any eight branches of agriculture; (c) define agro-allied industries as used in Week 6 and give five examples; (d) state five agricultural raw materials and their industries; (e) state ten relevance of agriculture; (f) define agronomy, crop pathology and entomology.

**Answer:** (a) Agriculture is the art and science or management of cultivating crops and rearing animals for human use, including production and handling of plant and animal products. (b) Any eight of the following: 1. Animal Science/Animal Husbandry/Animal Production: Ensures effective management of farm animals and better animal productivity. 2. Veterinary Medicine/Animal Health: Provides better health management for farm animals and controls pests, parasites and animal diseases. 3. Soil Science: Maintains soil fertility, supports soil classification and mapping, and helps control erosion and conserve soil moisture. 4. Crop Production/Crop Science: Supports effective crop management and the development/use of better planting materials and correct crop spacing. 5. Agricultural Economics and Farm Management: Supports effective management of agricultural resources, agricultural marketing and farm accounting. 6. Agricultural Engineering: Promotes mechanisation, develops processing and storage equipment, and maintains farm tools and equipment. 7. Agricultural Extension and Education: Trains farmers, disseminates agricultural information and links farmers with researchers. 8. Forestry and Wildlife Conservation: Promotes conservation of forest products and wildlife and helps create a suitable microclimate. (c) In Week 6 block 482, agro-allied/agro-based industries are industries that depend on agriculture for raw materials. Five examples: Oil mill, Soap industry, Textile/ginnery, Brewery, Paper industry. (d) Cotton — textile industry. Wool — textile industry. Cocoa — beverage industry. Coffee — beverage industry. Rubber latex — tyre and foam industries. (e) Provision of food for people and farm animals. Supply of materials for clothing, such as cotton, fibre, hides and skins. Supply of materials for shelter and furniture, such as timber. Generation of income from the sale of crops and animal products and from agricultural wages. Employment for farmers, farm workers and people in agricultural finance and marketing. Supply of raw materials for industries, such as cotton, cocoa and rubber latex. Generation of foreign exchange through exports of agricultural products. Provision of a market for industrial goods used in agriculture. Development of rural areas through roads, electricity, water, schools and hospitals. Provision of facilities for recreation and tourism, including game reserves and horse-related activities. (f) Agronomy: the science of crop production and soil management. Crop pathology: the study of diseases affecting crops and other plants. Entomology: the scientific study of insects.

**Marking:**
- 2 mark(s) — Defines agriculture as crop cultivation and animal rearing for human use.
- 16 mark(s) — Lists and defines eight correct branches: one mark for each branch name and one for its function.
- 2 mark(s) — Defines agro-allied industries according to Week 6 block 482 as industries dependent on agricultural raw materials.
- 5 mark(s) — Gives five valid examples from Week 6 block 486.
