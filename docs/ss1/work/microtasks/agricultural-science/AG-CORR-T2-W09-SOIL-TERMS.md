## Finding and correction

The source teaches the five factors of **soil formation**: climate, parent material, topography, biotic factors and time. In the topography section, “sloppy area” is a typographical error for **sloping area**. In the biotic section, “rock formation” is the wrong process; the context is **soil formation**.

Added `CORR-AG-T2-W09-SOIL-TERMS-REPLACEMENT`, binding the original row to `SUP-SS1-AGRICULTURAL-SCIENCE-T2-W09-SOIL-FORMATION`. The replacement explains all five factors, soil horizons, slope/runoff/erosion and the contribution of roots, soil animals and microorganisms. Original source bytes remain unchanged.
