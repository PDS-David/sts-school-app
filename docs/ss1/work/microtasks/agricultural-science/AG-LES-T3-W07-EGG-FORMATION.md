## Implementation

Title: **Egg formation in the laying hen: stages and approximate timings (Week 7 clarification supplement)**

The lesson:
- explains the ovary/ovulation distinction and the sequence of the five main oviduct regions;
- describes fertilisation in the infundibulum when viable sperm are present;
- explains albumen formation in the magnum, shell membranes in the isthmus and hard-shell formation in the uterus/shell gland;
- gives qualified hen-specific reference timings: infundibulum 15–17 minutes, magnum about 3 hours, isthmus about 75 minutes, shell gland about 20–21 hours, vagina a few seconds to less than one minute, total about 25–26 hours;
- states that these are approximate domestic-hen values, not universal timings for every bird species;
- includes five keyed practice questions.
