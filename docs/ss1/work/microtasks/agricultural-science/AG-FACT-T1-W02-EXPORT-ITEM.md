## Evidence on yam exports

### Japan International Cooperation Agency (JICA), April 2024
*Data Collection Survey on Agriculture Sector in Nigeria (QCBS), Final Report, Annex 1*, Yam section (pp. 16–20):
- The report states that imports and exports of Nigerian yams are few, not nonexistent.
- Its Yam trade section says that most Nigerian-grown yams do not meet importing-country quality requirements, which limits exports.
- It identifies OFI Foods and Olu-Olu Foods as exporters of pounded-yam flour to the United Kingdom, the Netherlands and the United States.
- The yam value-chain diagram includes an export branch and an export quantity marker for 2018.
- The report says it was prepared by the JICA study team using sources including FMARD APS, FAOSTAT and Comtrade.
- Source: [JICA report (PDF)](https://openjicareport.jica.go.jp/pdf/12376091.pdf), Yam section around PDF pages 16–20; extracted report lines 653–665 and 778–780.

### Nigerian Export Promotion Council (NEPC), 13 June 2024
- NEPC's article “Senate Committee on Trade and Investment visit the NEPC Headquarters” discusses yam export and the need to improve yam-export quality to reduce shipment rejections.
- The same article frames the discussion in the context of non-oil exports and foreign-exchange generation.
- This is direct evidence from Nigeria's export-promotion agency that yam export is an actual activity, not merely a theoretical possibility.
- Source: [NEPC article](https://nepc.gov.ng/blog/2024/06/13/senate-committee-on-trade-and-investment-visit-the-nepc-headquarters/).

### Federal Ministry of Information and National Orientation, 21 July 2026
- The Federal Ministry's report on NEPC's Benue export interventions says NEPC established Yam Export Clusters to improve production, quality, traceability, aggregation and access to international markets.
- This confirms that yam is an identified non-oil export product in current Nigerian export-development policy; the cluster announcement is supporting context, not a shipment statistic.
- Source: [FMINO report](https://fmino.gov.ng/nepc-to-strengthen-benues-non-oil-export-sector-through-strategic-interventions/).

### United Nations Statistics Division commodity classification
- UNSD HS 2012 code 071430 explicitly classifies yams (`Dioscorea spp.`) as a commodity traded internationally, fresh, chilled, frozen or dried.
- The classification confirms the relevant product category for international trade; by itself it does not establish Nigeria's export flow.
- Source: [UNSD HS 071430 classification](https://unstats.un.org/unsd/classifications/Econ/Structure/Detail/EN/32/071430).

## Finding
**Yam is not a defensible answer to the question as currently worded.** Nigeria does export yam and yam products, although JICA characterises the trade as small and constrained by quality requirements. NEPC's direct discussion of yam-export shipments and export rejects corroborates that the trade is real. An export, even a small one, can earn foreign exchange; the question does not define a minimum scale for “source”.

The fact that “yam” is absent from Week One block 104 only means it is not one of the examples listed there. It does not prove that yam is not exported. The current wording incorrectly turns an incomplete example list into a claim about national export status.

## Recommendation — no teacher decision presumed
1. **If the question is intended to test factual export status:** withdraw Q4 as currently worded. The offered options do not provide a defensible “except” answer merely because yam is omitted from block 104.
2. **If the intended objective is recall of the Week One examples:** rewrite it narrowly as: “Which of the following is not named in the Week One list of agricultural exports?” With the existing options, the answer to that *different* question would be **B. yam**. This tests recall of the source list and does not claim that yam is not exported.
3. **If the owner wants to retain a factual export-status question:** define a reporting period and replace an option only after checking official Nigerian/UN trade records for that period. Do not key yam as a non-export without such evidence.

No answer key was changed and no teacher/owner decision is presumed. No numerical export-value claim is used in the recommendation: available secondary aggregations give inconsistent yam trade values, and a precise figure is unnecessary to establish that exports occur.
