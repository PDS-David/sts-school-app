# SS1 French — Third-Term Week 1 audio acquisition requirements

## Exact source requirement

The registered Third-Term source, Week One, contains the two instructions:

- “Refrence a une cassette”
- “Reference a une C.D selon WAEC ET NECO.”

The source does not identify an examination year/session, paper number, track number, recording title, publisher, source URL, language track, narrator or actual audio file. No matching recording is included in the original source archive.

## Minimum evidence required before acquiring/installing audio

The owner or teacher must identify:

1. Exam board: WAEC or NECO (or both, if two recordings are intended).
2. Subject and examination year/session.
3. Paper, section and track number/title, if applicable.
4. Publisher/source and a stable source reference or the original physical media.
5. Confirmation that the selected recording is the intended French listening/dialogue practice.
6. A copy of the file in an application-supported format (MP3 or WAV) or a permitted source from which the project can obtain it.
7. Rights evidence permitting the school/app to store, process and make the recording available to its intended learners. Public accessibility alone is not proof of reuse permission.

If more than one recording is intended, provide a separate identity and rights record for each.

## Safe alternative already authored

`SUP-SS1-FRENCH-DIALOGUE-T3` contains an original dialogue, speaker roles, reading instructions and written comprehension. It also explains that a teacher may read the dialogue aloud while learners listen without looking at the text. This is an original equivalent activity, not the requested WAEC/NECO recording and not a claim that oral/listening performance has been observed.

The owner may explicitly approve:
- using the original dialogue and teacher-read activity as the complete replacement for the unidentified cassette/CD requirement; and
- omitting the unidentified recording from the deployable subject assets.

Until one path is approved, `FRENCH-EXAM-AUDIO` remains a delivery blocker. No audio asset is registered or installed.
