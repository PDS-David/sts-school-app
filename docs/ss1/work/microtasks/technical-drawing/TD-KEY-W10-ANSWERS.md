## Keyed answers and marking

### Q01 — Radius and tangent (2 marks)

The radius drawn from the centre of a circle to the point of tangency is perpendicular (90°) to the tangent line.

### Q02 — External tangency of two circles/arcs (2 marks)

For external tangency, the distance between centres is the sum of the radii. Thus 150 + 25 = **175 mm**.

### Q03 — Connecting arc externally tangent to two circles (3 marks)

For a required arc radius 150 mm and given circle radii 25 mm and 20 mm:
- First construction locus radius = 150 + 25 = **175 mm**.
- Second construction locus radius = 150 + 20 = **170 mm**.
- The intersection of the two loci is the centre of the required connecting arc.

### Q04 — Internal containment feasibility (2 marks)

A circle of radius 20 mm cannot contain a circle of radius 25 mm. For internal tangency with one circle enclosing the other, the enclosing radius must be greater than the contained radius. The stated configuration is geometrically impossible.
