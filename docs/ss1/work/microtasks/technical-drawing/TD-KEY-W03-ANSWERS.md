## Verified calculations and methods

- For a regular n-sided polygon: exterior angle = 360°/n; interior angle = 180°−360°/n.
- Pentagon: 72°/108°; hexagon: 60°/120°; octagon: 45°/135°; nonagon: 40°/140°.
- The source's octagon construction by quadrant bisection produces eight vertices at 45° intervals.
- A regular hexagon inscribed in a circle has side length equal to the circle radius.
- The common-base two-triangle method uses 45° and 60° base-angle triangles to locate square and hexagon centres, then interpolates centres for other regular polygons.
