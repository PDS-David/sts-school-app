## Amendment/version control

- PLAC's consolidated Constitution is explicitly updated through the First, Second, Third, Fourth and Fifth Alterations; its third revision is dated October 2023 (front matter lines 0–5 and 32–36).
- The Ministry of Interior Citizenship Requirements PDF (published May 2025) cites sections 26(2A) and 26(2B) at lines 21 and 35 and lists great-grandparent evidence at line 27.
- Those subsection references do not appear in the 2023 consolidated Constitution's section 26 text (lines 970–983). The discrepancy must be reconciled against an enacted Gazette/Act before use; a Ministry portal reference alone is not enough to label an amendment enacted.
- The National Assembly Joint Committee's June 2026 report lists the 2025 spouse-citizenship and citizenship-by-investment proposals as Constitution Alteration Bills (Bill 18 and Bill 19, report pages 90–93 / extracted lines 1440–1495). The report describes a bill/consultation process, not an enacted amendment. Do not teach those proposals as current law without a later assent/Gazette check.
