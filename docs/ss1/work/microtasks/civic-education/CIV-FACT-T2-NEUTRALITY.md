### L279 and L283 — ethnic/party hegemony as a cause

They are interpretive and contested generalisations, not self-proving facts. They must be attributed to the source's analysis if discussed; they must not be taught as an established, universally accepted causal account. Any later factual treatment would require dated primary evidence and a clearly bounded claim, not an unsupported generalisation about an ethnic group or political party.
