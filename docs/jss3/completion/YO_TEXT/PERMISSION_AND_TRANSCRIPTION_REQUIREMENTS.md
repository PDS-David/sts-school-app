# YO-TEXT — what a passage needs before it can be taught

Nobody has been contacted. Contact needs the owner's authorisation (see OWNER_DECISION.md).

## 1. Identify the passage
1. Genre: Ṣàngó pípè, Ọya pípè or iwì egúngún, as named by the source itself.
2. Source: title, author, year, page and line range; or performer, place, date, occasion and recording.
3. Collector and transcriber, if different from the performer.

## 2. Transcription
1. Yoruba with full tone marks and under-dots, one performed line per line.
2. Words that could not be heard or read are marked, never guessed.
3. Checked against the original page or recording by a second Yoruba reader (name and date).
4. Compare against page images or the recording, not against a website copy.

## 3. Translation
1. Named translator; line-aligned with the Yoruba.
2. Say whether it is literal or free. Untranslatable words stay and are explained in commentary.

## 4. Reuse basis (one of)
1. Public domain: author's death year, edition, and who edited or translated it, with their status.
2. Open licence: the licence text as printed on the work itself, and confirmation that the licensor owns the passage (a licence on an article does not cover chants it reprints from others).
3. Written permission naming: the exact passage; display to enrolled students in the school app; attribution wording; whether adaptation (excerpting, notes) is allowed; who to contact if the holder withdraws consent. For a living chanter or community, their consent as well as any publisher's.

## 5. Records the repository needs
1. Source file or letter kept privately; the repository stores only its SHA-256 and metadata.
2. A `jss3Attributions.json` record for the lesson (credit, creators, licence, URL, source hash, changes, lesson body hash).
3. Quoted text and new commentary labelled separately inside the lesson.

## 6. What the lesson then contains
1. Attributed passage (or the selected lines, with omissions marked).
2. Vocabulary and cultural explanation in the school's own words.
3. Comprehension and analysis questions, answers and a marking guide.
4. A note that performance needs observed assessment and is not marked from text.
5. Tests: attribution record matches lesson identity and body hash; every question has an answer; quoted lines equal the checked fixture.
