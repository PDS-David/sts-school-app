## 1. JSS3-C01 — what the passage needs

From the register entry: one named passage of at least three lines with (1) tone-marked Yoruba, one performed line per line; (2) a named translator and line-aligned English; (3) a named collector or published locator (title, year, page or line range); (4) a reuse basis covering that passage, meaning an open licence printed on the work whose licensor owns the passage, or written permission naming the exact passage and display to enrolled students in the school app; (5) either access to the source host or the source file supplied privately, so its SHA-256 and locator can be recorded.

The register records one concrete lead: Ifayemisi Elebuibon, *Orisa Songs*, an Ọya oríkì whose first line begins "A sere dun ni oko", seen only in a search extract (page and edition unknown), which needs that rights holder's written permission. `NEXT_ACTIONS.md` N1 also names a separate, unexamined lead cited on page 2 of the Zenodo PDF; an attempt to open it on 29 October failed, and the PDF's licence does not cover it. The Zenodo questionnaire and its saying are not substitutes. Nothing was sent to anyone.
