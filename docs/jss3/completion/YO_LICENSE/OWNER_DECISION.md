# YO-LICENSE — owner distribution decision (prepared, not decided)

Nothing here is legal clearance. It states what the licence text says and the exact choice needed.

## Facts checked
- NOUN ENG 105 Module 2 front matter (read via page text, 21 Oct 2026): CC BY-SA 4.0; asks for "First produced by the National Open University of Nigeria", the NOUN logo and the publication cover; developer Oyeniyi Okunoye; cover photo credit Henry Ude; published 2021. NOUN's OER policy (§6.1, §7.1) also applies CC BY-SA 4.0 and requires NOUN to be stated as licensor. The policy has no separate logo/trademark rule.
- CC BY-SA 4.0 legal code: §3(a)(1)(A)(i) attribution "in any reasonable manner requested by the Licensor" (so the logo/cover request is plausibly part of the condition, not courtesy); §3(b)(1)-(3) adapted material must carry a BY-SA licence, include its text or URI, and carry no extra terms or Effective Technological Measures restricting the recipient's rights; §2(a)(5)(C) the same for the licensed material itself.
- Kolawole (2023): article page says CC BY-SA 4.0; PDF front matter says only "Creative Commons Attribution License". The stricter reading is followed.

## The one real question
SUP-YO-ORAL-TEXTS delivers adapted/selected excerpts (omissions marked, notes added). The app delivers lessons only to logged-in students, with term-PIN and sequence locks. Can that delivery be reconciled with §3(b)(3)/§2(a)(5)(C), which forbid restricting recipients' exercise of the licensed rights?

## Choose one
- **A. Keep the excerpts under BY-SA.** Label the lesson text (excerpts and the lesson's own commentary about them) CC BY-SA 4.0 in the app, accept that recipients may reuse it on those terms, and accept that login/PIN gating applies to the app, not to a right to reuse the text. Requires your confirmation, ideally with a lawyer's, that gating is acceptable.
- **B. Reference-only for the excerpts.** Keep the lesson, the objectives, the technique notes in the lesson's own words, the exercises and the links; replace the six NOUN lines and seven Ifá lines with a pointer to the page/lines in each source. Teaching coverage stays; the verbatim text goes. Needs a lesson-body change, so YO-TEXT and the schedule/implemented-lessons records must be regenerated.
- **C. Identified public-domain or permission-based equivalent.** Johnson (1921) is an unexamined lead; written permission from NOUN or the chanter/author is the other route.

Until you choose, nothing is removed. Option A or B does not remove the need to display the NOUN cover and logo (see REPORT.md).
