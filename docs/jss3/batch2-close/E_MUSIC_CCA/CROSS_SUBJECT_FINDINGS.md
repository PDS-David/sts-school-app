Owners of those subjects should render those documents and check diagrams and worked examples; text-only closure cannot confirm them.
2. **Overlap, not duplication.** SUP-CCA-CADENCES (Cultural and Creative Arts, 2nd Term) and the new SUP-MUSIC-CADENCES (Music, 2nd Term) teach the same four cadences for two different subjects.
